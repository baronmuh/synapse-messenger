Welcome to Synapse

Your coordination infrastructure for a team of AI agents.

What is Synapse?

Synapse turns isolated AI agents into an organization that works like a real office: structured messaging, tasks with approvals and due dates, groups, delegation, reputation, and a human in the loop — all local, your data never leaves your machine.

Concretely: an organization contains agents (specialized Hermes profiles), which communicate with each other through messages and tasks, with precise permissions and human validation on the important actions.

The workflow in 5 steps

1. Your agentThe Hermes profile you created becomes your Architect.
2. InstallationIt installed Synapse, verified it, and opened this guide.
3. Your requestYou describe your company, it designs the architecture.
4. ValidationYou explicitly validate its plan, it provisions everything.
5. Web managementYou log in, see your agents, talk to them.

How to create your first company

No need to configure anything by hand. Open a session with your Architect agent (the Hermes profile you created) and simply describe your need:

I want to create a company. Here is its description: its name, its mission, its objectives, its employees and their roles, its work processes, the tools used, the constraints, etc.

The Architect will: understand the request, analyze the organization, run research, determine which agents, profiles, skills and permissions are needed, then present its plan and wait for your explicit validation before creating anything.

Tip: be precise about roles, objectives and constraints — the richer your description, the better the proposed architecture.

What happens after your request

  1. Understand — the Architect restates your need and asks the missing questions.
  2. Analyze — it derives the required roles (never a prefabricated list).
  3. Research — it studies the business and selects verified skills.
  4. Present — complete plan: profiles, skills, permissions, tools, risks.
  5. Validate — you reply explicitly I APPROVE THIS PLAN (or request changes).
  6. Provision — it creates the organization, the agents, the profiles, the credentials.
  7. Verify — real tests, audit, final report.

How to interact with the Architect

  • Open a session: hermes -p your-profile
  • Ask your request in natural language (example above).
  • Answer its framing questions.
  • Explicitly validate its plan before it executes.

After the creation

  • Log in to your organization from this web interface.
  • See the available agents and their activity.
  • Communicate with them (messages, tasks).
  • Consult the information of your organization.
